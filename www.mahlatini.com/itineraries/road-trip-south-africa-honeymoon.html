Road Trip South Africa Honeymoon

Inspirational self drive honeymoon to South Africa

This honeymoon is a showcase all that South Africa’s beautiful Cape province has to offer. Road trip through the exquisite scenery visiting Cape Town, the Cape Winelands, the Garden Route and concluding with an exhilarating safari in the Eastern Cape. Start in Cape Town staying the chic Camissa House, at the base of Table Mountain, offering spectacular views of the city and Atlantic Ocean.

Spend your days exploring the city’s most famous sights from Table Mountain and the V&A Waterfront to the penguins at Boulder’s Beach and your evenings on the rooftop bar watching the sunset. Then take a slow drive through to the food and wine capital of South Africa that is the Cape Winelands. Staying at the exquisite Boschendal Wine Estate you can enjoy their picnics or go wine tasting on the famous Franschhoek Wine Tram. Then take the cliff-hugging coastal road to the De Hoop Nature Reserve where you can soak up the sun on a secluded beach and look out for whales. Plettenberg Bay, on South Africa's famed Garden Route, is your next stop, with its many action-packed activities and beautiful beaches. More adventure beckons as you end your journey in the Eastern Cape staying at the tranquil Kwandwe Great Fish River Lodge in the private Kwandwe reserve. Fully immerse yourself in nature and enjoy day and night game drives in search of the Big 5.
