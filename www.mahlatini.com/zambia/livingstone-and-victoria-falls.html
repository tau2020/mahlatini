Explore Livingstone & Victoria Falls

Often considered a 'must-see' in Zambia, Victoria Falls is world renowned for its scenic beauty and natural majesty. The Falls and their surrounding area play host to a variety of thrilling activities, ultra-luxe accommodation, and unforgettable experiences. Set along the border of Zambia and Zimbabwe, both sides have plenty to offer and afford different, but equally impressive, views of the mighty waterfall.

Highlights

  • Witness one of the 7 natural wonders of the world
  • Swim in the adrenalin-pumping Devil's Pool
  • Enjoy a unique perspective with The Flight of Angels over the Falls
  • Thrilling adventure activities, including bungee jumping & canoeing

Iconic Activities

  • Boat Trip
  • Helicopter Flights
  • White Water Rafting
  • Cultural Experience
  • Canoeing/Kayaking
  • Fishing
  • Horse Riding
